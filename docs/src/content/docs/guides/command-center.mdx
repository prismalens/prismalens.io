title: Command Center
description: Your operational dashboard for monitoring incidents and investigations.
---

The Command Center is the primary operational dashboard in PrismaLens. It provides a real-time view of all active incidents, ongoing investigations, and items needing attention.

## Layout

The Command Center uses a **master-detail** layout:

- **Left panel** — List of active incidents with severity indicators
- **Right panel** — Details of the selected incident

## Quick Stats Bar

At the top, clickable metric cards show:

- **Active Incidents** — Currently open incidents
- **Running Investigations** — AI investigations in progress
- **Pending Alerts** — Alerts not yet correlated to an incident
- **Services Affected** — Count of impacted services

Click any metric to drill down to its filtered view.

## Needs Attention

A highlighted section surfaces items requiring human action:

- **Unassigned alerts** — New alerts not yet linked to incidents
- **Pending recommendations** — AI suggestions awaiting approval
- **Mapping issues** — Alerts that couldn't be mapped to a service

## Real-Time Updates

The Command Center updates automatically via polling. Active investigations show live progress as AI agents work through their investigation steps.
