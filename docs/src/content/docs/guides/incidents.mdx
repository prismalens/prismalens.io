---
title: Working an incident
description: The board, the incident page and the conversation with the agent. What starts an investigation, where a message goes, how to stop one, and how to reopen an incident.
---

This page follows one incident from the board to a finished investigation. It covers
what each screen shows, what starts an investigation, where your text goes,
and how to stop or reopen.

## The three screens

**The board** is what **Incidents** opens on. It has four columns:

| Column | What sits there |
| --- | --- |
| Needs you | Something waits on a person: an alert nobody acknowledged, an investigation that failed, or a resolved incident waiting to be closed. |
| Working | An agent is investigating right now. |
| Concluded | Open, with nothing running and nothing waiting on you. |
| Resolved | Closed. |

The sidebar lists every incident, grouped by service. An incident appears
under its own service, or under its first alert's service when it has none.
Closed incidents wait in **Settled** at the bottom, folded until you open it.
Alerts and Settings are the icons at the foot of the sidebar. The button at
the top left, or **[**, hides the sidebar and brings it back.

**The incident page** opens when you pick one. Under the title bar is a row
of tabs: **Overview**, **Conversation**, **Report**, **Alerts** and
**Timeline**. The right end of that row says what the latest investigation is
doing. Overview is a stack of cards: Investigation, Conclusion, Needs you (only
when something does), Alerts, Timeline and Details. A card shows its first few
rows; its link opens the matching tab. **Esc** goes back to Overview.

**The conversation** shows the agent's messages, its tool calls grouped into
one line each, and the box to write to it. **Ledger** in its header switches to
the row-per-event view. Each press of **Investigate** starts a new
investigation; the Investigation card lists the incident's others, and each
opens in the Conversation tab.

## The incident's status and the investigation's state are different things

The title bar shows the incident's status: Triggered, Investigating,
Identified, Monitoring, Resolved, Closed. That is where the incident is in its
life, and a person sets it.

The right end of the tab row shows what the agent is doing:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Starting | The agent is being started in a fresh copy of the service's code. |
| Working | Events are arriving. The strip names the current step and the time since it started. |
| Stopping | You asked it to stop and it has not stopped yet. |
| Stopped by you | You stopped it. It is not a failure. |
| Failed | The investigation ended with an error, shown on the Investigation card with the path to its transcript. |
| Done | The investigation finished and wrote a report. |

An incident can read **Investigating** while no agent runs. The strip is what
tells you whether one is working. When no step has arrived for 90 seconds, the
strip says how long it has been, for example `no step for 2m 10s`.

## Who reads what you type

There are three places to type, and each has one reader:

| You write | Where | Who reads it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Context for an investigation | The box, before an investigation starts | The agent, at the end of its first instructions |
| A message | The box, while an investigation is live | The investigationning agent, in the same session |
| A note | The Timeline card | People only. No agent ever reads the timeline. |

Nothing you type starts an investigation on its own. Only **Investigate** does: the
button in the title bar, the one in the box, or dropping a card on **Working**.

While an investigation is live, the box offers two ways to send:

- **Enter** queues the message. The agent reads it when its current turn ends.
  Today one turn is the whole investigation, so a queued message is answered
  just before the report.
- **Ctrl+Enter** sends it now. The agent stops the step it is on and reads
  your message straight away. Anything that step had started is lost.

Your message shows **Queued**, then **Delivered**, then **Answered**. If the
investigation ends before the message reaches it, it shows **Not delivered** and offers
**Save as note**.

Here is a live incident with one queued message, as the conversation shows it:

```
INC-12  p99 latency on checkout-api above 1.2s for 10m       Investigating   41m ago
Working  Claude Code  Opus 5.5  read src/cache/stampede.ts   4m 12s          Stop

You  14:27                                                           Delivered
Focus on the cache layer; the TTL changed this morning.
Ran 3 tools  read 2 files, searched 1 pattern
The p99 rise starts at 14:02, two minutes after deploy 9f3c1a.
You  14:31                                                           Queued
Check the deploy at 13:58 first; the TTL change shipped in that one.
Thinking 12s
```

## Stopping an investigation

**Stop** sits at the right end of the tab row whenever an investigation is live. It asks first. The agent
stops at its current step, and everything it found so far stays in the
conversation. The strip then reads **Stopped by you**, and the timeline gets
**Investigation stopped**. A stopped investigation does not put the incident in
**Needs you**.

## After an investigation ends

A finished investigation cannot be asked anything more: its session is closed. The box
switches to starting a new investigation, and what you type there is context for that
investigation. **Investigate** works on any incident, resolved and closed ones
included. Investigating a resolved or closed incident does not change its
status.

## Reopening

A resolved incident can be reopened from the title bar's **…** menu, or by
dropping its card on **Concluded**. It goes back to Investigating and its
resolve time is cleared. The next resolve sets a new one. Reopening does not
start an investigation. A closed incident stays closed; you can still
investigate it again.

## Dragging cards on the board

A drop does the one thing its column means, and asks first when that thing
costs something:

| Drop on | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Working | Opens a small form to pick the agent and add context, then Investigate. |
| Concluded | From Working: stops the investigation. From a resolved incident: reopens it. |
| Resolved | Resolves the incident, after asking, and offers to stop a live investigation first. The card then waits in Needs you until someone closes it. |
| Needs you | Nothing. That column fills itself. |

The same actions have buttons on the incident page. The board does not drag
on a phone.
