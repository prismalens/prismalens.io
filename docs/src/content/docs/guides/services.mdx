The Services page is your living service catalog. It maps your infrastructure, tracks dependencies, and controls how PrismaLens investigates incidents per service.

## Service Tiers

Every service is assigned a tier that controls its investigation behavior:

| Tier | Auto-Investigate | Response Target |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Critical** | Always | Immediate |
| **High** | Always | 15 minutes |
| **Medium** | On-demand | 1 hour |
| **Low** | Manual only | Best effort |

## Service Types

- **Gateway** — API gateways, load balancers
- **Service** — Backend microservices
- **Database** — PostgreSQL, Redis, etc.
- **Queue** — Kafka, RabbitMQ
- **Cache** — Redis, Memcached
- **External** — Third-party APIs

## Service Detail Tabs

### General
Name, description, owner, type, tier, and contact information.

### Integrations
Which monitoring tools are connected to this service and how alerts are mapped.

### Investigation
Per-service investigation policy: auto-investigate toggle, preferred models, custom prompts, and approval requirements.

### Dependencies
Upstream and downstream service relationships. Used by the AI during investigation to understand blast radius.

## Service Discovery

PrismaLens can automatically discover services from your connected integrations. When a new service name appears in alert labels, PrismaLens creates a placeholder entry for you to configure.

## Topology View

A visual graph showing service dependencies and their current health status. Click any service node to navigate to its detail page.
